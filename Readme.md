### About Me

I'm an AI Engineer based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, currently building **agentic AI systems** at
**Codemen Solutions Limited**. I architect multi-agent orchestration platforms, LLM/RAG pipelines,
and automation tools with persistent memory, business-document intelligence, and multi-format
content generation — serving 50+ enterprise and SaaS clients in production.

My core interests sit at the intersection of **Computer Vision, Deep Learning, and Explainable AI
(XAI)**. I hold a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from Ahsanullah University of Science
and Technology, and I've co-authored published research on explainable lung disease classification
from chest X-ray images.

- 🔭 Currently building **multi-agent orchestration systems**, RAG pipelines, and document-intelligence
  tools in production
- 🧠 Research interests: **Computer Vision, XAI, NLP, and Agentic AI**
- 🏆 Actively compete in ML/data science competitions on Kaggle and university-hosted hackathons
